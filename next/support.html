Getting started

Which devices does Trimly work on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, Mac with macOS 15 or later, and Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later. One purchase covers all of them.

Is there a subscription?

No. Trimly is a one-time purchase on the App Store.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in. If you use iCloud, your edits sync through your own iCloud account.

Opening videos

Where can I open videos from?

Your Photos library, the Files app, external drives, and network shares over SMB or WebDAV. You can also share a video to Trimly from another app, or drag files onto Trimly on Mac and iPad.

Does Trimly make a copy of my video?

No. Trimly reads the original where it already is, so a long recording never takes up space twice.

The one exception is a video shared to Trimly from another app on iPhone or iPad. That one is copied, and you can review and delete those copies in Settings ▸ Imported Copies.

Which formats can Trimly open?

Most video and audio files your Apple device can play, such as MOV and MP4 video and common audio formats. Trimly also opens raw PCM recordings; it asks you for the sample format when you open one.

Why is a video from Photos slow to open?

If the original is stored in iCloud, it has to download first; you can cancel the download at any time. Videos you have edited in Photos can also take a little longer, because Photos prepares the edited version before Trimly can read it.

Editing

Can I cut several clips from one video?

Yes, as many as you like. Give each clip a label, and capture stills or Live Photos at any frame.

Can Trimly find the good parts for me?

Find Clips searches the recording on your device: what was said, sounds such as laughter or music, people and animals, text on screen, highlights, and the beat of the music. GoPro HiLight and DJI highlight marks appear too. You look through what it found and choose what to keep.

Exporting and Photos

Why do my clips show up on the right day in Photos?

Trimly works out when each clip was really filmed from the original’s capture time, and saves it with that time, along with the location, camera and lens details when the original has them.

My travel clips show the wrong time. What can I do?

Set a time zone shift in Settings, and exported clips are saved with the local time where you filmed. If the camera’s own clock was wrong, set the correct capture time for that file, and every clip from it is corrected.

Does exporting reduce quality?

With transcoding set to Off, Trimly keeps the original picture and sound, and export is almost instant. A LUT, a crop or some rotations need the video to be re-encoded; choose Quality, Balance or Efficiency to trade quality against file size.

Can I take my selects into Final Cut Pro or another editor?

Yes. Export FCPXML or EDL for your editor, and SRT for subtitles.

LUTs and Log

Which LUTs are built in?

Rec. 709 conversions for Apple Log 2, DJI D-Log and D-Log M, GoPro GP-Log and Insta360 I-Log. You can also import your own .cube files, and they sync to your other devices through iCloud.

How do I see my graded Log footage in Photos?

Open the video from Photos and choose a LUT. With Settings ▸ Save LUT to Photos turned on (it is by default), Trimly saves the graded look to that video in Photos. The original Log is kept.

How do I remove the look again?

Choose No LUT for that video in Trimly. On a Mac you can also use Revert to Original in Photos.

Can I grade many videos at once?

Select several videos on Trimly’s home screen and apply a LUT to all of them. To grade a whole album, use the Save LUT to Photos action in Shortcuts.

Can I apply a LUT inside the Photos app?

Yes. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, Trimly adds a Photos editing extension that applies a LUT to a video right in Photos, for videos Photos lets you edit. For Log footage, use Save LUT to Photos instead.

Sync and privacy

What does iCloud sync?

Your clips, labels and other edits, and your LUT library. The videos themselves are never uploaded.

Can I start on one device and finish on another?

Yes. Your edits sync through iCloud, and Handoff lets you carry on from the next device. If the other device can reach the same file, through Photos, iCloud Drive or the same NAS, it opens where you left off.

Is my footage analysed in the cloud?

No. Find Clips and every other analysis runs on your device using Apple’s built-in frameworks. See the Privacy Policy.

NAS and network shares

Can I edit videos stored on my NAS?

Yes, over SMB or WebDAV. Trimly plays and cuts straight from the share, without downloading the whole file first.

Where are my NAS passwords stored?

In your device’s Keychain.
