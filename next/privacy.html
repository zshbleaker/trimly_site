Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

  • No accountNothing to sign up for.
  • No trackingNo analytics, ads or telemetry.
  • On deviceYour footage never leaves it.
  • iCloud: edits onlyNever your videos.

Trimly processes your media entirely on your device, and your footage stays on your iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV. Edits and LUTs can sync across your Apple devices through iCloud, but video files are never uploaded. This policy explains, in plain terms, what that means for your privacy.

No data collection

Trimly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no telemetry of any kind.

iCloud sync

Trimly has no servers of its own, and it does not connect to any third-party service. The only network activity is iCloud sync: when you turn it on, Apple’s iCloud keeps your edits and LUTs in sync across your devices, under Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy. Video and audio files are never uploaded or synced.

Files and Photos access

When you open media from the Files app, external storage, a network share, or your Photos library, Trimly reads it only so you can trim and export it. All processing happens on your device, and exported clips are saved where you choose. Trimly accesses your Photos library only with your permission: to open the media you select, to save the clips you export, and, when you ask it to, to save a LUT look to a video in your library.

Network shares

When you add an SMB or WebDAV share, Trimly connects only to the address you enter. The password is stored in your device’s Keychain.

No account, no third-party network access

Trimly has no account system and no sign-in. Apart from Apple’s built-in iCloud sync and the network shares you add, it makes no network connections, and it does not use any analytics service or cloud processing.

AI features

All of Trimly’s AI features run on frameworks built into Apple’s systems. Trimly doesn’t include any third-party AI models, and never will. It doesn’t share your data with any third-party AI provider. Everything runs locally on your device, under the same privacy rules that apply to AI features across Apple’s systems.

Children’s privacy

Because Trimly collects no data at all, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
