Changelog

What’s new in Trimly

Every version since the first release, newest first.

Draft — 1.8.1 is the published App Store text. Earlier entries are drafted from the commit history; please review before publishing.

1.8.1Oct 2026

Widgets, time zones and multi-select

What’s new

  • Adjust the time zone used for exported files
  • Added desktop widgets
  • Improved Shortcuts support
  • Select multiple files and sort the file list

Bug fixes

  • Fixed an issue where videos imported from Photos could disappear from the library after restarting
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when opening PCM audio from a network location
  • Refined the interface and improved overall stability

1.8Oct 2026

Camera highlight marks and on-screen text

What’s new

  • GoPro HiLight and DJI highlight marks appear in Find Clips and on the timeline
  • Find Clips can search text that appears on screen
  • Transcripts are embedded as subtitle tracks in exported videos
  • Spatial audio is kept through rotation and export, with a warning when an export would flatten it
  • Capture time and place follow the changes you made in Photos
  • Skim through a video by hovering over its filmstrip
  • File details show where the file came from and what was exported

1.7Sep 2026

Spotlight

What’s new

  • Find your Trimly files in Spotlight
  • Trimly actions appear in Siri suggestions

1.6Jul–Sep 2026

Apple TV, network shares and Find Clips

What’s new

  • Trimly on Apple TV
  • Open and cut videos on SMB and WebDAV shares
  • A new home screen grid, with cassettes for audio and vinyl for lossless files
  • Export FCPXML and EDL for your editor
  • Find Clips brings together highlights, music analysis, subjects, transcripts and sounds in one place
  • Smart thumbnails and automatic clip labels
  • Export only the clips you select
  • Crop, straighten and correct perspective
  • Trim points snap to speech, silence and the beat
  • Stickers in Messages

1.5Jul 2026

On-device transcription

What’s new

  • Transcribe speech and translate it, on device
  • Add clips straight from the transcript, and save it as SRT
  • Recognise sounds and find subjects
  • Clip labels are written to Photos captions
  • Long-press to speed up playback, and swipe to change the volume

1.4Jul 2026

Shortcuts

What’s new

  • Shortcuts actions for trimming and exporting
  • Export straight from the file list
  • Faster exports on Macs with Max and Ultra chips
  • Reveal exported files in Files on iPhone and iPad

1.3Jun 2026

iCloud sync and Handoff

What’s new

  • Edits and LUTs sync across your devices through iCloud
  • Continue on another device with Handoff
  • AirPlay to your TV or Mac
  • A LUT Manager for all your LUTs
  • iPhone landscape layout
  • More built-in LUTs, including DJI
  • A timeline overview you can drag to move around a long video

1.2Jun 2026

Filmstrip timeline

What’s new

  • A filmstrip on the timeline, and a timeline you can resize
  • Colour bars for every clip
  • Haptic feedback while you edit
  • Export clips without sound

1.1Jun 2026

Camera LUTs and HDR

What’s new

  • Built-in LUTs for GoPro, DJI and Insta360
  • HDR LUTs, and HDR stills and Live Photos
  • Clips keep the original video’s metadata
  • Save exports to an album in Photos
  • Open videos from Photos without making a copy
  • Playback speed controls and the share sheet

1.0Jun 3, 2026

First release

What’s new

  • Cut as many clips as you like from one video
  • Capture stills and Live Photos
  • Save to Photos or Files
  • Waveform timeline with frame-by-frame stepping and undo
  • Apple Log LUT and your own .cube files
  • Rotation, audio files and raw PCM recordings
